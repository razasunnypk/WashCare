# WashCare Roadmap

## Phase 1
Orders, pricing engine, cash, receipts, daily reporting, customer/item catalogue, payments, offline operation, backup/restore, audit history.

## Phase 2
Expenses, staff, attendance, inventory, stock movements, suppliers and advanced operational reporting.

## Phase 3
Card/digital payment integrations, reconciliation and loyalty program.

## Phase 4
Customer PWA, pickup requests, driver interface, delivery tracking and notifications.
